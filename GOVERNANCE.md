# Governance

Quanta is maintained and directed by its founder, Ata Türkçü ([yoitsata@gmail.com](mailto:yoitsata@gmail.com)), who has final say over the project's direction and every decision described below. This document exists so that's stated plainly rather than left implicit.

## Project Direction

The roadmap, scope, and priorities of Quanta are set by Ata. Discussion and proposals from contributors are welcome and genuinely considered, but the final call on what Quanta becomes rests with him.

## Code and Pull Requests

Anyone can open a pull request. Whether it gets merged, and in what form, is Ata's decision -- he reviews contributions against the project's direction and the standards in [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md), and can request changes, decline, or merge at his own discretion.

## Release Management

What ships, when it ships, and how it's versioned is decided by Ata.

## Decision-Making

This project does not currently operate by committee, vote, or consensus process. Where this document doesn't say otherwise, the decision is Ata's to make.

## A Living Document

Quanta is early, still a small project. This governance model reflects that stage and may change as the project and its contributor base grow. Any such change will be reflected here.
